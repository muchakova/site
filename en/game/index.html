Marina vs the Tax Zombies

The last week of the quarter. Tax zombies pour out of the archive and pelt citizens with audits, fines and penalty notices. People don’t know what to file, by when, or where.

Only one person knows the Tax Procedure Code, the VAT Act and the Personal Income Tax Act by heart. Marina grabs her coffee, her cape and her code book and heads out into the street.

  • MoveArrow keys or WASD. With a mouse or finger: press and drag.
  • AttackMarina throws § sections at the nearest zombie on her own.
  • RescueWalk through a citizen and they leave reassured.
  • DocumentsThey can’t hurt Marina. If she catches one, she appeals it.
  • EspressoGrab the coffee and the sections fly twice as fast.
2 levels and a final boss · you can’t lose

Controls: ← ↑ → ↓ or WASD. With a mouse or finger: press and drag. Marina fires on her own. Pause: P or Esc.

Frequently asked questions

How does the consultation work?

Online, on Google Meet, at the time you chose. We go through your case and finish with concrete next steps.

Which consultation should I choose?

30 minutes is enough for one clearly defined question. If you have several income types, foreign accounts or a company, choose 60 minutes.

How do I pay?

By card when you book. Payment is processed securely by Stripe. Prices are final.

Can I reschedule or cancel?

Yes, up to 24 hours before the meeting. Call me on +359 887 460 907. If you cancel, I refund the full amount.

What should I prepare?

A short description of your question, the years concerned, and your broker or exchange statements, if any.

It depends on what the meeting is for: an answer to your case, clear next steps, or guidance on filling in your tax return or document. If you like, also a recording of the conversation: just tell me in advance.

Yes. Invoices are issued by Vesta Consult. Email office@vesta-bg.eu with the details for the invoice and a phone number in case we need to clarify anything.
